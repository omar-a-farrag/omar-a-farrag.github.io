I am a research assistant in the International Finance Division at the Federal Reserve Board of Governors. I received a B.S. in Economics and a B.A. in Mathematics from UNC-Chapel Hill in 2024. After working two years in the [Emerging Market Economies](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/ifeme-staff.htm) section, I now serve as the RA to the division director, [Dr. Beth Anne Wilson](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/beth-anne-wilson.htm#professional). 

My primary research interests lie in firm behavior and competition, particularly with respect to innovation and health. I also hold secondary interests in macro modeling and trade.
